# RTX 4070 0.23.0 profile capture attempts

Two full-suite campaigns were started on RTX 4070 under WSL on
2026-11-27 UTC. Both used driver 610.88, a frozen pilot for 26 timed
modes, and the thermal and Windows desktop gates. Neither campaign
reached five valid rounds in every mode. Both stopped before rule 3.
The raw mode output, host telemetry, frozen schedules and stop
records remain archived with their respective campaigns.

## First campaign

The registered in-process host gate applied to integrated GPUs.
At the first measurement revision, `long_capture.py` also used
that gate to judge each RTX 4070 mode. The thermal gate passed after
256.4 s. The 57 desktop readings were 0% or 1% GPU utilization,
with decoder and encoder at 0%.

All 14 saved mode-rounds were void. Their host checks all returned
failed, with a peak system busy share of 99.9600% in `l2-merge`.
The recorded void reason was the host check for 13 rows. The
`spill-latency` row failed its dispersion check first, so it records
that reason. Its recorded rule 1 count was 5 with 3 allowed and
rule 2 count was 6 with 10 allowed. The operator stopped after
`sync-loop-multicta` completed at 22:42:10 UTC. The `launch` mode
had no saved round and no launch-overhead median to report.

[PR #37](https://github.com/VirtualGPU/VirtualGPU/pull/37)
(`18c2a3d`) aligned this check with the registered protocol.
The subsequent RTX 4070 run retained host telemetry and used it
for reporting, while mode eligibility used the registered checks.

## Second campaign

The second campaign used a new binary, required by the CUDA source
change in PR #40, and a new pilot and freeze. Three full rounds
recorded 78 mode-rounds, 72 valid and 6 void. Twenty-four modes
were valid in each round. The two exceptions are listed below.
Each had three voids, so neither could reach five valid rounds
within the seven-round limit. The operator stopped at 23:26:53 UTC
during the fourth round's gates, after the third round had fully
recorded its results. There was no fourth-round timed mode.

All three formal desktop gates passed. Decoder and encoder were 0%
throughout their traces. The release lead had minimized the windows,
turned off the display and suspended two interface webview process
trees before collection. The pilot and pre-capture desktop traces
each contained 57 readings, 0%-1% GPU and 0% decoder and encoder.

The release lead identified the recurring JVMs as belonging to the
local `kt-main-elasticsearch` container. Its restart policy was
`always`, with 1,944 recorded restarts and about 55 s between starts.
The lead stopped the container after collection. Round 1 recorded
Java, Go test processes and Python among its offenders. Rounds 2
and 3 also recorded Java. The Go tests were reported complete after
collection. The archived per-mode host summary records every
offender and its associated mode, SHA-256
`067203099b61646cebb4cf4c35aa6a80379fb3f4bc3fdcc87da82024c3353e8b`.
