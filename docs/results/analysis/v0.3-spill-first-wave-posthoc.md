# Post-hoc recheck of the first-wave spill footprint

This CPU-only recheck evaluates the landed implementation at `cd5f35f`.
The microbenchmark measurements are legacy short-launch inputs, so the
conclusion is provisional. The analysis leaves the frozen v0.2 conclusions
in force. The earlier spill residency registration at `e7fed3a` on
`s5/v03-spill-residency` describes the separate per-SM candidate `5d7262d`. Its phase-entry rule
differs from the landed rule.

## Landed model and legacy recheck

The landed model uses
`max(1, min(hardware_ctas_per_sm, ceil(grid_ctas / sm_count)))`
as its first-wave peak. It uses that count for the
frame footprint, the spill traffic split, and the reload lookup throughout
the kernel. The synthetic spill benchmark launches one CTA per SM. Its
128 KiB of frames previously paid for the eight-CTA allowance of 1 MiB.
The recheck uses the same carveout, spill scope, profile values and matched
zero-reload subtraction in both builds.

| Device | Points | Signed median, base to landed | MdAPE, base to landed | MAPE, base to landed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| GB10 | 4 | +209.6% to +67.6% | 209.6% to 67.6% | 183.8% to 54.9% |
| RTX 4070 | 4 | +70.7% to +38.0% | 70.7% to 38.0% | 67.2% to 35.0% |

All 2,799 other predictions are bit-identical because their IR has no
spill charge. The landed and per-SM candidates also agree bit for bit on
all 2,807 recheck points. The spill rows still overpredict extra time.
Scaling both local bandwidth estimates by factors of 2, 3, 4 and 6
leaves all four predictions on each device bit-identical. The model
assigns this fixture an 8 KiB shared-memory carveout and uses the 16 KiB
L1 miss curve. At the 128 KiB footprint this curve predicts a miss
fraction of 1.0 on GB10 and 0.9998 on RTX 4070, so the reload lookup
charges nearly all reloads at its L2 latency. The hardware carveout
for this fixture has not been measured. The old measurements lack the long-launch
five-round quality record required by the current rules. The first GB10
long-launch capture voided six of seven spill rounds on rule 1. The
recheck uses none of those rounds. [Refreshed baseline](v0.3-mechanism-recheck-baseline.md)
[First GB10 capture](v0.3-gb10-first-full-capture.md)

## Tail-wave limit

For an uneven launch, some SMs can hold fewer CTAs than the first-wave
peak. A tail can also have fewer CTAs per SM after a full wave. The peak
charge still applies, so the model can overstate the local frame footprint
and reload cost for those phases. In a synthetic full wave followed by one
CTA, with a 1 KiB frame and 128 reloads per thread, the landed model
predicts 18,391.6 ns per step of extra time on GB10 and 18,469.0 ns on RTX
4070. The per-SM candidate predicts 15,235.0 ns and 17,754.5 ns
respectively, a gap of 20.72% and 4.02% relative to that candidate.
This is a difference between models on a constructed input. There is
no corresponding hardware measurement.

Across the archived IR, the landed and per-SM models agree bit for bit on
timings or unsupported status for 2,176 of 2,208 rows. Five differ
nominally, 27 differ only in perturbed draws, and all statuses agree. The
maximum nominal and any-draw differences are 0.308 ns (0.0000288%) and
170.765 ns (0.01806%), respectively. The raw comparison and synthetic
witness are in `results/v03-spill-cd5f35f/review.tar.gz`, SHA-256
`14215be75eea1378fdaabc74f429a8bd4ace2538539fd4ac42a104ad3a65fdf7`.
