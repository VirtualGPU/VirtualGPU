# Same-device check of the measured non-MMA issue rate: GB10

**Not decided.** The [registration](v0.3-non-mma-same-device-check-registration.md) judges a GPU on its first qualified 0.23.0 campaign, one in which every mode passed. The GB10 campaign behind `profiles/gb10-0.23.0.json` (`8450ba9`) failed rule 3 in `spill-latency`, so the profile's spill fields are carried over from the earlier profile, and the campaign does not qualify. The GB10 verdict waits for a campaign in which every mode passes. The two modes this check reads, `mma` and `sfu-shfl`, passed all three rules in that campaign (rule 3: 0 of 13 and 0 of 31 points).

The registered script was still run on that campaign, as agreed before it was run. **Every number below is descriptive and is not the registered verdict.** Whether the eligibility should change to "the modes the check reads passed" is put to the author of the criterion in #20, with these numbers disclosed; this document changes nothing in the registration.

## Descriptive result (not the registered verdict)

The field came from pure FFMA (`cuda_fp32_fma_unrolled`, 8.5734 Gwarp-inst/s per SM at a mean clock of 2514 MHz). The mix peaks at 16 warps.

| Kernel | Warps | Measured | Reading B: predicted, miss | Reading A: predicted, miss |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Pure FFMA (source of the field) | 16 | 8.5734 | 8.5734, +0.0003% | 2.902, -66.2% |
| FFMA and LOP3 mix (peak) | 16 | 7.8681 | 8.5734, +8.96% | 7.640, -2.9% |

Gwarp-inst/s per SM. Under reading B both misses are at most 10%; the script prints `pass`. The bound for a source slower than the field is `1 / 1.1`, 90.9% of it; the mix reached 91.8%.

The script prints a verdict from its inputs only: it checks that the profile carries `capture_protocol` and a measured field, not that every mode of the campaign passed. Its `pass` here is therefore not the registered decision, which is not decided for the reason above.

Reported, not judged:

- Per MHz: the mix measured 0.003169 Gwarp-inst/s per SM per MHz against 0.003410 for the field, a miss of +7.6% under reading B.
- The mix curve under reading B, miss by warp count: 1 warp +345%, 2 warps +122%, 4 warps +12.1%, 8 warps +10.6%, 16 warps +8.96%, 32 warps +9.18%. The core does not limit issue by the warp count, so the low counts miss widely, as the registration expects.
- Reading A misses FFMA by the per-step stage synchronization on a barrier-free loop, as the registration describes; the mix, with 128 instructions per step, is less affected.

## What would decide it

A GB10 0.23.0 campaign in which every mode passes, run through the same script and inputs of the same kind. The numbers here say only where this campaign would have landed; they do not stand in for that run.
