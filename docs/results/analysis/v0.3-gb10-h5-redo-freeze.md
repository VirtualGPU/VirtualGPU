# H5 on GB10: freeze of form (a) before the capture

Status: written and committed after the pilot and before any capture round, as [the registration](v0.3-gb10-h5-redo-registration.md) and [its amendment](v0.3-gb10-h5-redo-amendment.md) require. GB10 captures form (a) only (`h5-dram`).

- **Measurement code.** `fe8e2d7`: the `h5-dram` mode and the launch schedules. Binary built on Spark 1 with `microbench/build.py --arch sm_121a` (CUDA 13.0), SHA-256 `7c48760b3567f43c6a4bebb3d0f9eae114c881b0e586bfc49a692c56c4d39259`.
- **Scoring code.** `0aa6ff6`, which differs from `fe8e2d7` only in `validation/h5_redo.py` and its tests: it freezes and scores `h5-dram` without an `h5-staging` pilot.
- **Pilot.** Spark 1 under the GPU lock, 2026-11-27 22:58:20 to 22:58:32 UTC.
  - `microbench/long_capture.py pilot ... --modes h5-dram`.
  - Manifest SHA-256 `89e7b436...`; pilot output `36dfa6c4...`.
  - All 325 points fit their CTAs per SM.
  - The fastest pilot launch was 3.00 to 5.81 ms for every DRAM point (calibration point 3.02 ms) and 1.25 ms or more for every hot-L2 point. The largest grown request was 691 MiB.
- **Local memory.** `cuobjdump -res-usage` of the binary, demangled (SHA-256 `2bd23176...`), lists all 71 narrow kernel instances H5 uses, each with stack 0 and local 0. No point uses local memory, and none is excluded.
- **Predictions.** [validation/h5-redo-gb10-frozen.json](../../../validation/h5-redo-gb10-frozen.json), written by `python -m validation.h5_redo freeze` from [profiles/gb10-0.23.0.json](../../../profiles/gb10-0.23.0.json) (SHA-256 `f5fa8ecc...`) and the core built at `71304a4`, whose compiled sources are those of `fe8e2d7`.
  - BW is 222.70 GB/s.
  - All 325 points are frozen at their pilot lengths: the calibration point, 192 DRAM points, 96 hot-L2 points and 36 `sync-loop-narrow` points.
  - The calibration point's floor, the larger of T_core and the sector limit, is 1.54 ms.
