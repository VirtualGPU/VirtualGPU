# Miss merging under the round-trip port floor: registered candidate fixes

Registered on main `ac7c3b4` (core identical to `70de487`), before any candidate is implemented or evaluated, for review by 6 and release by the lead. Nothing below changes after a candidate is run. This document changes no core, profile or rule.

ROADMAP item: "Re-run miss merging under the round-trip port floor". The re-check on the GB10 0.23.0 profile ([v0.3-gb10-0230-mechanism-recheck.md](v0.3-gb10-0230-mechanism-recheck.md), section 2) confirmed the coupling. The criteria below read microbenchmarks only, not the development set.

## Facts the candidates rest on

The core today (`port_floor` in `crates/vgpu-core/src/sim.rs`), with `own` the bytes of one round trip over the port (`load_bytes / round_trips / l2_bw(1.0)`) and `shared = own * resident`:

- L2 latency: `max(lat.l2, l2_idle + own, shared)`
- DRAM latency: `max(lat.dram, dram_idle + own, shared)`

`latency_parts` charges the DRAM latency to every byte not resident in L2, merged or fetched. Only fetched bytes enter DRAM in flight (`dram_fraction`), so a kernel whose misses mostly merge has a low DRAM in flight and a low `lat.dram`, and the floor binds.

Per-point signed error of `l2_merge_by_sharing` (stagger 0, profile `gb10-0.23.0`), core `9c12720` (before the floor) and main `70de487`. Here `r` is the number of CTAs that read the same lines:

- **What the floor adds grows with sharing and with bytes per CTA.** The gap is largest at one CTA per SM (16 to 48 CTAs on GB10's 48 SMs). The summary "fewer CTAs than SMs" in the ROADMAP is a consequence: the 48 CTA, r = 16 points move nearly as much as the 16 CTA ones (up to 31.8 points against 38.3).
- **Reading.** A merged round trip is charged the full DRAM idle latency and then the streaming of all its bytes through the port, in series. That the data fit better without the series sum is a measured fact. That the two overlap is the hypothesis the candidates test.

## Candidates

Each is a change to the DRAM line of `port_floor` only. None has a parameter, and none reads a new profile field. The L2 line and `shared` are unchanged in every candidate.

| Name | DRAM latency | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| F0 (control) | `max(lat.dram, dram_idle + own, shared)` | main today |
| F1 | `max(lat.dram, shared)` | the per-round-trip streaming floor holds for L2 only; a DRAM round trip is bounded by the MLP curve and the SM's port share |
| F2 | `max(lat.dram, dram_idle, l2_idle + own, shared)` | streaming from L2 to the SM overlaps the DRAM latency of the first line; a DRAM round trip lasts at least the longer of the two |
| F3 | `max(lat.dram, dram_idle + own * g, shared)`, `g = dram_fraction / (1 - resident_fraction)` | only fetched bytes stream behind the DRAM latency; merged bytes ride on fills already under way (`g = 1` when nothing merges, or when no byte waits DRAM latency) |

F3 is identical to F0 when no miss merges, so it leaves every `r = 1` point exactly as it is.

## Evaluation

- Core: main `70de487` plus each candidate, one at a time, built clean. Profile `profiles/gb10-0.23.0.json` unchanged.
- Tool: `scripts/recheck_mechanisms.py` unchanged, all its mechanisms, draw 0, with the tool's existing noise column (median span of the five round medians).
- Baseline: F0 as in the re-check (main `ac7c3b4`).

## Criteria, fixed here

A candidate **passes** when all of these hold against F0:

1. **Merging improves.** `miss_merging` MdAPE falls by more than its noise (0.63 points, from 4.9%), and its MAPE falls (from 8.8%).
2. **Unshared points do not move.** On the 12 `r = 1` points, no signed error changes by more than 0.5 points.
3. **The floor's basis holds.** `port_floor_serial_fill` and `port_floor_sync_loop` each: MdAPE worsens by at most 0.5 points and MAPE by at most 1.0 point.
4. **Nothing else worsens.** `closed_loop_dram`, `closed_loop_l2`, `closed_loop_mma`, `lsu_pipe` and `dependent_chain` each: MdAPE worsens by at most 0.5 points. Carried-over rows (`spill_l1_boundary`) are not judged.

**Choice.** Among passing candidates, the lowest `miss_merging` MdAPE wins. Candidates within 0.63 points of the lowest count as tied, and a tie goes to the first in the order F3, F2, F1 (smallest change to what the core charges first).

**No candidate passes.** The core keeps F0, and the coupling is written into design.md 9.3 as a known limit with the table above. This is the draft recommendation of the preregistration.

## Second device

The RTX 4070 has no qualified profile, so the choice is made on GB10 alone. When a qualified RTX 4070 profile exists, the chosen form is re-run there with criteria 1 to 4 unchanged. A failure there reopens this item and does not revert the core by itself; that call is the lead's.

## Limits

- Stagger 0 only: KernelIR has no per-CTA start offset, so the 16-stage stagger points are not checked.
- One profile on one device.
- The candidates change the DRAM latency of every kernel whose DRAM floor binds, not only merging ones. Criteria 3 and 4 cover the microbenchmarks that exercise it; kernels outside them are not covered.

## Result: F3 is chosen

Evaluated after this registration was merged (#35). Each candidate was committed on a local evaluation branch from `a077475`, whose compiled sources equal `70de487`: F1 `4399b56`, F2 `16e4b93`, F3 `1fc8099`. Each was built clean (`result_class` formal). `scripts/recheck_mechanisms.py`, unchanged, ran on `profiles/gb10-0.23.0.json` at draw 0. F0 is the re-check on main `ac7c3b4`. The criteria were applied by a script that encodes the four rules and the choice above ([criteria.json](v0.3-port-floor-merge/criteria.json)).

- **All three pass, and they tie on merging.** All three give identical `miss_merging` errors on every point, so the tie rule applies and F3 is chosen.
- **F3 is the only candidate that changes no other mechanism.** F1 and F2 change 67 `closed_loop_dram` and 17 `closed_loop_mma` predictions, and move `closed_loop_dram` from 0.71% to 0.82% MdAPE (MAPE 1.74% to 1.95%), within criterion 4. F3 changes 23 of the 51 `miss_merging` predictions and leaves every other prediction of the re-check bit for bit at its F0 value.
- **The merging points under F3 equal the pre-floor core `9c12720`.** The signed median by CTA count is +2.4, +3.0, +4.0 and +4.0% at 16, 23, 48 and 96 CTAs. The worst point before, +35.0% at 16 CTAs, r = 16, 16 KiB, is now -3.3%. The new worst point is -6.6% at 48 CTAs, r = 16, 16 KiB.
- **The RTX 4070 column is still pending a qualified profile.** On the short-launch profiles of the re-check baseline (comparison only, not a criterion), F3 gives `miss_merging` 7.8% to 4.6% on GB10 `0.22.0-posthoc` and 11.3% to 4.8% on RTX 4070 `0.21.0-posthoc`. Both equal the pre-floor values of [the baseline](v0.3-mechanism-recheck-baseline.md).

The core change is `e16e98c` on this branch. The development set is reported only after the choice, as registered, in the prediction diff of the pull request.
