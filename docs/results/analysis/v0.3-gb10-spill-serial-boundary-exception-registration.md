# GB10 spill-latency: device exception for the serial 1 KiB reload points (registration)

Status: registered before any capture under it, together with [the allocation design](v0.3-spill-allocation-design-registration.md); both are decided by that design's adoption check and are withdrawn together if it fails. Nothing below changes after the adoption capture is taken.

## Scope

- Device: GB10 only.
- Points: `spill_reload_latency_by_count`, `dependency_mode` `serial_chain`, `regime` `l1_boundary` (1 KiB frames per thread, 128 KiB per SM, the L1 size), reload counts 8, 32, 68 and 128: four points.
- Rules: the four points leave rules 1 and 3, as the `low_in_flight_unstable` exception does (long-launch-capture.md, section 4): they are removed from those rules' lists and points, and from their counts, before the check. Rule 2 judges every list, these four included.
- The points stay in the capture and the profile, each with its `extra_ns_vs_zero` as before, carry a flag naming the exception, and store the minimum and maximum of their per-round medians.
- No consumer reads them: the core's reload lookup reads only `batch_before_dep` points of the `l1_hit` and `l2_spill` regimes, and no core test or re-check reads serial points. The registered spill decision (`ad1d505`, test 2) reads the four `batch_before_dep` points at 1 KiB, which this exception does not cover; its author was told of this exception in issue #21.

## Evidence

From the first GB10 0.23.0 capture (main `42e039d`, seven rounds, raw archive SHA-256 `94f7717a...`) and the default-carveout arm of the carveout diagnostic (`gb10-spill-carveout-plan.md`, merged as `d406464`, three processes): ten processes, one local memory allocation each.

**Rules in the first capture, independent of any description chosen here:**

| Point (serial, 1 KiB) | Rule 1 (rounds of 7) | Rule 2 (rounds of 7) | Round medians (us) and their spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 reloads | 6 | 7 | 1469 to 2071, 37% |
| 32 reloads | 6 | 7 | 1369 to 1928, 38% |
| 68 reloads | 7 | 7 | 1303 to 1590, 21% |
| 128 reloads | 7 | 7 | 1409 to 1809, 27% |

They were all four of the capture's rule 2 failures in every round (4 of 9 allowed). The serial 1 KiB point without reloads never flags (interquartile range about 0, largest over smallest at most 1.04).

**Shape (a description chosen after the data):** the published two-cluster flag of the MLP capture report (a gap of at least 5% of the median and over 2.5 times the larger within-cluster interquartile range) flags these points in only 1 to 3 of the seven rounds, because their slow samples are spread widely. They are therefore described by the share of samples above 1.4 times the process's lower quartile and the ratio of the medians of those slow samples to the rest:

| Point (serial, 1 KiB) | Slow share per process (ten processes) | Slow over fast | Largest over smallest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 reloads | 0.00 to 0.53 | 1.59 to 1.95 | 1.46 to 2.45 |
| 32 reloads | 0.07 to 0.47 | 1.73 to 2.37 | 2.27 to 2.85 |
| 68 reloads | 0.07 to 0.40 | 1.54 to 2.38 | 1.69 to 3.01 |
| 128 reloads | 0.13 to 0.40 | 1.59 to 2.27 | 2.01 to 3.02 |

Each launch lands in a fast state or one about twice as slow, at random sample positions within one process and so within one allocation. An explicit maximum-L1 carveout does not remove it (the carveout diagnostic). Why it happens is not known; the frames are exactly the size of the L1.

**Not in scope:** the batched 1 KiB points. No sample of theirs is above 1.4 times the lower quartile in any of the ten processes; the points at 68 and 128 reloads are wide (interquartile range 6% to 13% and 5% to 18%, largest over smallest up to 1.37) and failed rule 1 in 4 and 6 of the seven rounds, never rule 2, with round medians spreading at most 8.1%. They are a different behavior and are judged by all rules; the risk they leave is stated in the allocation design's Limits.

## Adoption

Decided by the allocation design's adoption check, which judges rules 1 and 3 without these four points and rule 2 with them. If that check fails, this exception is withdrawn with the design.
