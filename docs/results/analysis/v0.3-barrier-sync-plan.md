# Plan: what one more barrier per loop step costs on GB10 (registration)

**Plan, not a result.** Registered before any code or capture, for two `ROADMAP.md` items: the core charges a stage synchronization (`sync_ns`) on every pipeline step, also on a loop with no barrier; and it charges one `sync_ns` per step whatever the number of barriers. It produces measurements only. It does not propose how the core should change; that is a separate pull request once this result exists.

## Why

`sync_ns` in the core (`Machine::sync_ns`, charged per `Phase::Main` step in `sim.rs`) is looked up in `pipeline.fixed_sync_ns` by warp count and buffer count. That value comes from `fixed_sync_empty_kernel`: one CTA (`grid = 1`), 4 or 8 warps, and per step one `cp.async.wait_group`, one `__syncthreads()` and one `cp.async.commit_group` with no other work (`profiles/gb10-0.23.0.json`: 26.0 ns at 4 warps and 29.2 ns at 8 warps, one buffer). So it is the cost of one barrier per step on an otherwise empty loop. The archived v0.2 kernels have 2 to 14 barriers per step, and the synthetic non-MMA kernels have none ([post-hoc look](v0.3-barrier-count-posthoc.md), [non-MMA registration](v0.3-non-mma-same-device-check-registration.md)). The post-hoc look could not separate the barrier count from head dimension and tile shape in kernel timings; this measures it directly, at fixed work.

## What is measured

A new mode, `barrier-step`. One kernel template, compiled per barrier count `B`: a loop of `steps` iterations; each iteration runs a fixed block of independent FP32 FMA work (8 chains per thread, as in `fp32_fma_kernel`) of `W` warp instructions per warp, then `B` plain `__syncthreads()` (`bar.sync 0`). A loop-carried counter keeps the loop alive at `B = 0` and `W = 0`. No memory access in the loop.

Points, every combination of:

- `barriers_per_step` `B`: 0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 14 (14 is the largest count in the v0.2 kernels).
- `work_per_step` `W`: 0, 64 and 512 warp instructions per warp: an empty step, as in `fixed_sync_ns`; a short step, about twice a barrier's cost at 8 warps on GB10's measured issue rate; and a step several times a barrier's cost.
- `num_warps`: 4 and 8 (the warp counts of `fixed_sync_ns` and of the v0.2 kernels).
- `grid`: 1 CTA (as `fixed_sync_ns` is measured) and one CTA on every SM.

72 points. Each point's value is the time per step: launch time over `steps`, from the long-launch protocol below.

Before any capture, the assembler output of every instance is checked: exactly `B` `BAR.SYNC` and `W` `FFMA` per loop iteration (after unrolling), and nothing else in the loop except the counter, compare and branch. An instance that fails is fixed before the capture; the check is reported with the result.

## Launch protocol

As every scheduled mode ([long-launch-capture.md](../../components/long-launch-capture.md)): a pilot sizes each point's `steps` until its fastest launch reaches 1.25 ms, the schedule is frozen with `B`, `W`, `num_warps` and `grid` as design fields, every launch is at least 1.0 ms, and `--clock-probe` records the SM clock around every point. 15 samples and 3 warm-ups per point. Spark 1 under the GPU lock, after the quiet-host gate, three processes in one session, each host-checked; a failed host check voids that process and it is replaced once.

## Quantities

For each `(W, num_warps, grid)` and each process, the per-step time `t(B)` at the six barrier counts, as the median of the point's samples. Over processes, a point's value is the median of the three process values.

- The one-barrier step cost `d1 = t(1) - t(0)`.
- The per-barrier increment `b`: the least-squares slope of `t(B)` over `B` = 1, 2, 4, 8, 14.
- Linearity: the largest absolute residual of that fit relative to `t(B)` at the same `B`.

## Frozen reading

Per `(W, num_warps, grid)`, in this order:

1. **Capture rules.** Rules 1 and 2 per process and rule 3 over the three processes on `launch_ns_samples`, with the usual tolerances. A failing point is reported, not dropped.
2. **A barrier-free step does not pay the barrier cost** when, in all three processes, `t(1) - t(0)` is above both 5% of `t(1)` and half of the profile's `fixed_sync_ns` for that warp count and one buffer. **It pays it** when, in all three, `t(1) - t(0)` is below 5% of `t(1)`. Otherwise: not resolved.
3. **The barrier count matters** when, in all three processes, the slope `b` is positive and `t(14) - t(1)` is above 5% of `t(1)`. **It does not** when, in all three, `|t(14) - t(1)|` is below 5% of `t(1)`. Otherwise: not resolved.
4. **Linear in the count** (read only where reading 3 says it matters) when the largest residual of the fit is at most 5% of `t(B)` in all three processes; otherwise not linear, and the shape is reported.

Each reading is stated per configuration; the plan draws no single verdict across them. A configuration where the work hides the barriers (readings 2 and 3 negative at `W = 512` but positive at `W = 0`) is reported as such.

## Reported without a verdict

- `d1` and `b` against the profile's `fixed_sync_ns` (26.0 ns at 4 warps, 29.2 ns at 8 warps, one buffer).
- The same quantities in SM cycles, from the clock probes.
- One CTA against all SMs.

## Not covered

- Barriers next to asynchronous copies (`cp.async.wait_group`), and named or partial barriers.
- More than one CTA per SM, and other warp counts.
- The RTX 4070: the same plan applies later, under the quiet desktop gate.
- How the core should charge barriers.
