## Readings

Values are GB/s per SM, the median of the three processes' estimates (each the mean of 15 per-allocation medians).

1. **Capture rules.** As implemented, a point fails when any of its 15 allocation lists in any process has an interquartile range above 10% of its median or a largest over smallest above 1.5, or when its three process estimates spread more than 10%. Seven points fail:
   - the 4 B store points at 1, 2, 4 and 8 warps (the 1-warp point exceeds the interquartile limit in 13 to 15 of its 15 allocations per process; the others in 1 to 7);
   - all three L2 points (1 to 3 of 15 allocations per process, interquartile ranges of 10% to 18%).
   No point's estimates spread more than 5% between processes. Over the rank-mean lists, rule 1 fails 2, 1 and 1 of 21 lists in the three processes (1 allowed); rule 2 fails none.
2. **Saturation.**
   - 4 B loads: 18.7, 37.4, 62.1, 116.0, 143.7 and 143.8 at 1 to 32 warps. **Saturated** (16 and 32 warps within 0.1%), **value 143.8**.
   - 16 B loads: 53.7, 105.2, 172.4 and 263.3 at 1 to 8 warps. **Not saturated** (4 and 8 warps differ by 35%): 263.3 is a lower bound.
   - 4 B stores: **not resolved** (four of its points fail a rule).
3. **Frame independence in L1.** The 8-warp 4 B loads at 32, 64 and 128 B frames read 115.5, 115.6 and 116.0, within 0.3% of their median: **clean**.
4. **Tiers: not resolved** (the three L2 points fail a rule).
5. **Allocation.** All 4 B and 16 B L1 loads are **allocation-independent** (spans at most 1.4% in every process). All six 4 B store points and the two larger L2 frames are **allocation-dependent** (store spans 9% to 27%, L2 spans 9% to 32%); the 16-warp 1 KiB L2 point is independent (spans 3.6% to 4.9%). The allocation-dependent points' uncertainty covers half their pooled span: 8.5% to 15.5% for the stores, 9.0% and 16.7% for the 2 KiB and 4 KiB L2 points.
6. **One rate for loads and stores: not resolved** (the store curve is not resolved).

## Reported without a verdict

- Against the estimates: the saturated L1 4 B load value, 143.8, is 4% below the estimated `local_mem_l1_hit_bw_per_sm_gbps` (150) and 45% of `smem_bw_per_sm_gbps` (321). Wide (16 B) loads reach at least 263 at 8 warps.
- The 4 B stores run at 14 to 20 for every warp count, far below the loads, and vary with the allocation; the L2 points run at 37 (16 warps, 1 KiB), 24 (8 warps, 2 KiB) and 12 (4 warps, 4 KiB), against the estimated 40 for `local_mem_l1_miss_bw_per_sm_gbps`.
