# Static local loads per causal attention loop (S1): result

**Post-hoc, compiler output only.** This is step 1 of [the local load count plan](v0.3-local-load-count-plan.md). It reads compiled SASS, no label and no GPU. It fits nothing and changes no core, frontend or profile.

- **Tool.** `scripts/static_local_loads.py`, run at commit `0b2ad76` (the same script as `7c6f0a0` on this branch after the rebase), with Triton 3.9.0 and its bundled `ptxas` (`sm_89`) and `ptxas-blackwell` (`sm_121`).
- **Scope.** All 108 configs of the four causal development cases of `validation/attention-suite.json`, on both targets. No holdout case is compiled.
- **Data.** [sm_89](v0.3-local-load-s1/s1-sm_89.json) and [sm_121](v0.3-local-load-s1/s1-sm_121.json).

## Method

Each config is compiled through the frontend's AOT path. Its PTX is assembled with the frontend's `ptxas` flags plus `-lineinfo` and disassembled with `nvdisasm -gi`. Each `LDL` and `STL` is then classified twice: by loop, and by where in the loop it sits.

- **Loop.** An instruction belongs to the main or the diagonal loop when its inline stack has a frame on that loop's `_attention_tiles` call site in `fused_attention`. Otherwise it is outside both loops.
- **Loop body or peeled.** It is in the loop body when it also lies within the address range of a backward branch. Otherwise it is peeled: prologue and epilogue copies of a pipelined loop, or the helper's setup.
- **Width.** It comes from the opcode suffix (4 B unless `.64`, `.128` or a narrow type).

The per-iteration estimate weights the two parts by the loop's trips, counted from the shape as `microbench.spill_counters` counts them. With V CTA iterations and E loop entries over the launch, the estimate is (body x (V - E) + peeled x E) / V. It assumes a peeled copy runs once per loop entry. That assumption is not checked.

**Check of the parse.** On both targets and all 108 configs, the bytes of all `LDL` and `STL` in the SASS equal the assembler's `spill_loads_bytes` and `spill_stores_bytes` exactly.

## Results

Spilling configs: 27 of 108 on `sm_89` and 28 on `sm_121`. `m64n32-w8-s3` of `b2-q32-kv4-s768-d128-causal` spills on `sm_121` only.

S1's per-iteration estimate against the core's figure per loop (`spill_loads_bytes / 4 / spill_loop_count`), over the spilling configs:

| | `sm_89` | `sm_121` |
|---|---|---|
| Main loop, S1 over core: median (range) | 0.13 (0.00 to 0.86) | 0.31 (0.01 to 0.63) |
| Diagonal loop, S1 over core: median (range) | 0.40 (0.00 to 0.86) | 0.31 (0.00 to 0.45) |
| Main over diagonal, where both reload: median (range) | 1.17 (0.01 to 2.26) | 0.99 (0.02 to 2.04) |
| Share of a launch's `LDL` instructions outside both loops: median (range) | 0.14 (0.03 to 0.83) | 0.17 (0.03 to 0.95) |
| Share of `LDL` instructions peeled from the loop bodies: median (range) | 0.17 (0.04 to 0.43) | 0.21 (0.04 to 0.57) |
| `LDL` wider than 4 B, all scopes | 4 of 4935 | 540 of 5317 |
| Predicated `LDL` | none | none |

1. **The core's figure overcounts every spilling config on both targets.** On no config does S1 reach the core's per-loop figure; the highest ratio is 0.86. The largest absolute cases are the `m128n128-w4` configs: the core charges 322 to 366 reloads per loop, and S1 gives 173 to 238 in the main loop and 96 to 127 in the diagonal one.
2. **The two loops hold different spill code.** In the `m128n128-w8` configs the main loop has almost no reloads (0.0 to 0.3) and the diagonal loop has 1 to 8. In the `m128n128-w4` configs the main loop reloads about twice as much as the diagonal one.
3. **A part of the static figure is outside both loops, or peeled.** By instruction count, the median is 14% (`sm_89`) and 17% (`sm_121`) outside, and 17% and 21% peeled. In the `b1-q16-kv16-s1025-d64` configs most reloads are outside both loops (S1 is 0.0 to 3.6 per iteration against a core figure of 2.5 to 21.5).
4. **Wider reloads are almost all on `sm_121`.** There, 540 of 5317 `LDL` are 8 B, against 4 on `sm_89` (peeled copies of the main loop of `m128n32-w4-s2/s3`). Where reloads are 8 B wide, bytes over 4 B overcount the reloads.
5. **The targets differ.** The same configs spill differently on `sm_121` and `sm_89`. Of the 27 configs that spill on both, 23 have more spill load bytes on `sm_121`. The ratio is 0.99 to 8.6, the largest for `b1-q16-kv16-s1025-d64` `m128n64-w4-s2` (172 B against 20 B). A count made for one target does not carry over to the other.

**Orientation only, not a reading.** Three configs of `b2-q32-kv4-s768-d128-causal` were also compiled for `sm_86`. They give the same S1 as `sm_89`. Against the post-hoc RTX 3070 counters ([spill-counters-rtx3070-posthoc.md](spill-counters-rtx3070-posthoc.md)):

| Config | Loop | S1 | RTX 3070 counters |
|---|---|---|---|
| `m128n64-w4-s2` | main | 32.0 | 32.4 |
| `m128n64-w4-s2` | diagonal | 16.0 | 23.5 |
| `m128n128-w8-s2` | main | 0 | 0 |
| `m128n128-w8-s2` | diagonal | 3.0 | 3.0 |

The plan sets this comparison aside: the RTX 3070 is not a reference GPU, and the readings of step 3 use RTX 4070 counters only.

## What this does not settle

- **S1 against executed counts.** The agreement of S1 with D1 is step 3 of the plan. It needs counters on the RTX 4070, and on GB10 root access that the project does not have. Until then S1 on GB10 stays unchecked.
- **The peeled weighting.** One peeled copy per loop entry is an assumption, and the diagonal loop of `m128n64-w4-s2` is the case where it gives 16.0 against the RTX 3070's 23.5.
- **No model change.** Using S1 in the core waits for the spill decision of `ad1d505` to be scored, as the plan states.
