# H5 on GB10: form (a) under the long-launch protocol (result)

Status: the result of [the registration](v0.3-gb10-h5-redo-registration.md) as [amended](v0.3-gb10-h5-redo-amendment.md), with predictions frozen before the capture ([freeze](v0.3-gb10-h5-redo-freeze.md)).

**Verdict: form (a), the DRAM-side narrow request form, does not pass on GB10.** The capture qualifies and the MAPE meets the limit, but the aligned 2 B group's median error is -10.7%, beyond the 10% group limit. As amended:

- form (a) does not go into the core in v0.3.0, and no further GB10 attempt is registered;
- form (b) keeps its existing GB10 verdict on `stage-narrow-k1-2`, not passed.

The RTX 4070 half is registered separately.

- **Shortest launch.** 1.25 ms.

## Form (a)

The calibration point's per-round medians were 3.054, 3.094, 3.044, 3.064 and 3.022 ms, median 3.054 ms. Its floor is 1.540 ms, so R is identifiable: R = 3.51 warp requests per ns. Earlier GB10 sessions gave 3.39 and 3.50 at a shorter length, in void captures.

| Group | Points | Form MAPE | Current model MAPE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Pure loads from DRAM (pass rule) | 192 | 9.6% | 25.3% |
| `sync-loop-narrow` DRAM (reported only) | 36 | 12.7% | 22.2% |
| Hot L2 (regression) | 96 | unchanged | 24.2% |

Median error by width and alignment, pure loads from DRAM:

| | 1 B | 2 B | 4 B | 16 B |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Aligned, form | +1.2% | **-10.7%** | +0.6% | +5.2% |
| Offset, form | +0.7% | -1.0% | +0.5% | +2.1% |
| Aligned, current model | -51.8% | -17.0% | -6.6% | -2.0% |
| Offset, current model | -51.8% | -37.1% | -23.3% | -7.9% |

The pass rule requires all four of: R identifiable (yes), MAPE under 15% (9.6%, yes), no width and alignment group with a median error beyond 10% (aligned 2 B at -10.7%, no), and unchanged hot-L2 predictions (yes). Form (a) does not pass.

## Reported, not judged

- The 24 aligned 2 B points range from -26.5% to +3.1%; the p90 of the absolute error over all 192 points is 17.5%.
- In the second void GB10 session of form (a), the aligned 2 B group was also the one beyond the limit, at -10.9% ([h5-followups-gb10-posthoc.md](h5-followups-gb10-posthoc.md)). That capture is void and was not used here; it is noted only because the same group missed again.
- The hot-L2 regression points are not changed by the form, as required. Their current-model group medians were -11.7% to +10.8%.
