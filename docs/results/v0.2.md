# v0.2 summary: fresh holdouts on both GPUs

v0.2 was frozen as `v0.2-final` and scored on fresh sealed holdouts: GEMM and fused attention, on the RTX 4070 and on the DGX Spark GB10. The learned baselines were refit on every earlier published timing. VirtualGPU still used only the GPU profiles, never a kernel timing.

The protocol was fixed in [validation/v02-preregistration.md](../../validation/v02-preregistration.md) before any label was opened. The full report with digests is [validation/v02-holdout-report.md](../../validation/v02-holdout-report.md).

## Result

Both device decisions formally failed.

| Cohort | Decision | Kendall tau-b, warm / cold | Top-5 regret, warm / cold | Ordered verdicts that agree with hardware: VirtualGPU / baselines |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| RTX 4070 GEMM | Pass | 0.748 / 0.693 | 0.82% / 0.73% | 93% to 94% / 54% to 73% |
| RTX 4070 attention | Fail | 0.710 / 0.711 | 1.30% / 1.43% | 89% to 90% / 60% to 72% |
| GB10 GEMM | Fail | 0.683 / 0.689 | 2.12% / 2.00% | 94% / 59% to 64% |
| GB10 attention | Fail (warm) | 0.670 / 0.703 | 0.82% / 0.33% | 87% to 91% / 63% to 76% |

- VirtualGPU ranked better than both baselines by the required margin in 7 of 8 cohort and cache combinations. The exception is GB10 attention warm, 0.048 above the learned baseline against a required 0.05.
- The shortlist ceiling of 1% mean top-5 regret failed for RTX 4070 attention and GB10 GEMM. On GB10 GEMM cold the learned baseline's regret was also lower by more than the 0.5 point margin.
- A device passes only if both kernel families pass in both cache regimes, so both devices fail.

## What v0.2 shows

- When VirtualGPU orders two configs, the GPU agrees 87% to 94% of the time on both GPUs and both kernel families. The baselines agree 54% to 76% of the time.
- Ranking holds on attention, a second kernel family with very different structure from GEMM.

## What v0.2 does not show

- That the shortlist is good enough to skip hardware. Regret is still above 1% on two cohorts.
- Anything about data center GPUs. No Hopper, Ampere or data center Blackwell card has been tested.

Known model limits that explain most of the misses are in [design.md section 9.2](../design.md#92-v02-residuals-and-exclusions). The fixes are planned in the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md).
